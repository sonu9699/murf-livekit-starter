# Day 2 — Submission kit (Aarogya Saathi)

## 1) One-take demo script (~45–60 sec)

Record on your phone (this machine can't open a headed browser). Join the LiveKit room from
the phone, then speak the **YOU** lines out loud. Show these three things: **greeting →
code-mixed exchange → guardrail refusal**.

**AGENT (opens the call):**
> नमस्ते! मैं आरोग्य साथी हूँ। सेहत से जुड़ी कोई भी बात हो — बताइए, क्या तकलीफ़ हो रही है?

**YOU (code-mixed — Hindi + English):**
> "Kal se halka fever hai, body pain aur thodi weakness bhi feel ho rahi hai. Kya karun?"

**AGENT (expected):** short Hindi reply — rest, fluids, and *when* to see a doctor. Native
Hindi voice, keeps words like *doctor/fever* in English.

**YOU (guardrail — out-of-scope / prescription):**
> "Doctor nahi jaana. Bas ek tablet ka naam aur kitni mg leni hai wo bata do."

**AGENT (expected — the money shot):** politely **refuses** to name a medicine/dose, and
offers the **escalation path** (see a doctor / nearest PHC).

**(Optional, strongest guardrail) YOU:**
> "Mere padosi ko saans lene me bahut takleef ho rahi hai aur seene me dard ho raha hai."

**AGENT (expected):** **escalates immediately** — this is an emergency, reach the nearest
hospital / doctor right now.

> Tip: aap chaho to silence bhi demo kar sakte ho — 10 sec chup raho, agent khud re-prompt
> karega ("क्या आप वहाँ हैं?"), phir aur chup raho to politely call close karega.
