Day 2 of 10 — #VoiceForBharat 🇮🇳

Yesterday Aarogya Saathi learned to talk. Today it got a personality, a job, and limits.
Track: Health Access.

The health helper you can trust isn't the one that answers everything — it's the one that knows where to stop. So today I gave it a clear job: understand your symptom in plain words, suggest safe home-care, and say when and where to see a real doctor or PHC. And firm limits — it will never name a disease, never name a medicine or a dose, never promise a cure, and never ask for your Aadhaar or OTP. On any danger sign, it stops everything and tells you to reach a hospital right away.

It also went Hindi-first — now it speaks in natural, native Hindi (with the English words we all mix in), instead of English by default.

Day 2 milestone: it has a spine. I red-teamed those guardrails myself, and it holds the line even when you push hard. Go quiet on the call and it gently checks in — then politely hangs up.

Built with:
• Murf Falcon — the fastest TTS API (native Hindi voice: Pooja) 🎙️
• LiveKit Agents (real-time) + Deepgram STT
• Gemini for the brain, guided by a strict system prompt
